The base visual stylesheet (now panel/aral.css) originated from the owner's
previous Blue Knight panel stylesheet and was extended for ARAL Proxy: a light
theme, RTL support, Persian typography (Vazirmatn, OFL-licensed) and the
multi-proxy components were added for this project.

The Charcoal & Silver theme layer additionally self-hosts two OFL-licensed
typefaces in panel/assets/: DM Serif Display (headings) and Bodoni Moda
(body text), both served locally — no third-party font requests.

The ARAL logos, backgrounds and brand identity in panel/assets/ were supplied by
the project owner specifically for this rebrand. Original artwork was resized and
optimized for web delivery (PNG/JPEG, 41–325 KB) without altering proportions.

The panel client, server, routing, protocol implementations and proxy-core
integrations remain project-specific code. MIT licensed, same as the base
repository.
